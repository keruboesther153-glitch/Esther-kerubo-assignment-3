        "## 10. Evaluate the model\n",
        "\n",
        "The project uses several classification metrics:\n",
        "\n",
        "- **Accuracy:** proportion of all predictions that are correct.\n",
        "- **Precision:** among observations predicted positive, the proportion that are positive.\n",
        "- **Recall:** among observations actually positive, the proportion correctly identified.\n",
        "- **F1-score:** harmonic mean of precision and recall.\n",
        "- **ROC-AUC:** measures discrimination using predicted probabilities.\n",
        "\n",
        "In a health-screening context, recall is important to examine because false negatives can be consequential. However, no single metric is sufficient for judging a real clinical system.\n"
      ]
    },
